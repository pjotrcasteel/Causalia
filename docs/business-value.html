When not to adopt

A smaller “no” is better than a vague “yes”.

Your system is mostly simple CRUD.

If meaningful correctness does not depend on timing, partial failure or concurrency, ordinary unit and integration tests will usually give a better return.

You need provider certification.

A deterministic model does not prove a real Kafka broker, database, Dapr sidecar or HTTP stack behaves exactly as expected. Use real integration tests for that.

Your main concern is throughput or latency.

Causalia.Load is for deterministic correctness under concurrency pressure, not a substitute for real performance and capacity testing.

You cannot state the business invariant.

If the team cannot say what must remain true, adding more schedule exploration may only generate noise. Start by defining the guarantee.
